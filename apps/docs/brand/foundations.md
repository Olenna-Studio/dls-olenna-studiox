# Brand Identity & Foundations

Olenna StudioX is a luxury multi-category design house encompassing beauty, fragrance, fine accessories, and haute couture. The visual language is **editorial rather than promotional**, characterized by warmth, restraint, and classical proportions.

## Brand Values

- **Everyday Elegance**: Luxury integrated naturally into daily rituals.
- **Editorial Poise**: Generous whitespace, refined serif headlines, and quiet confidence over noisy promotional callouts.
- **Thoughtful Curation**: Every product card, boutique detail, and digital interaction reflects bespoke care.
- **Sensory Tactility**: Warm ivory canvases paired with subtle champagne metallic accents and midnight plum depth.

## Brand Imagery & Creative Direction

- **Lighting**: Soft, natural golden-hour ambient illumination. Avoid harsh direct flash or cold fluorescent studio lighting.
- **Composition**: Centered or rule-of-thirds framing with substantial negative space.
- **Materials**: Natural textures (silk twill, cashmere, hand-blown glass, brushed brass, marble).
